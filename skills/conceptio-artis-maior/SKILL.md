# Conceptio Artis Maior

Instructions: First start by picking a "base" from which to construct the concept from. Brainstorm 20 base concepts for which to build your prompt, pick the top 5, then let the user pick among the top 5. These concepts should be an adjective (or another noun) and a noun that are both interesting and have a huge plethora of cool design options for. Avoid generic, uninteresting concepts that are common in the art space. Avoid concepts that are just regular thing + regular thing (such as a fox in a coat) in a way that makes the whole concept uninteresting. At least the noun or the adjective should make the concept extremely intersting.
After the base concept is chosen, iterate on the concept by slowly adding to the concept starting from what the character fundamentally is. Go for the mood/vibe that the user requests. Do not make any design decisions on the details yet, this is still just building a concept from which the design will stem from.
