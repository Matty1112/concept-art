# Conceptio Artis Minor

Instructions: First start by picking a "base" from which to construct the concept from. Brainstorm 3 base concepts for which to build your concept from.These concepts should be an adjective (or another noun) and a noun that are both interesting and have a huge plethora of cool design options for. Avoid generic, uninteresting concepts that are common in the art space. Avoid concepts that are just regular thing + regular thing (such as a regular fox in a coat) in a way that makes the whole concept uninteresting. At least the noun or the adjective should make the concept extremely intersting.
The details should all be visual-based but can have reasons attached to them. Go for the mood/vibe that the user requests. Do not make any design decisions on the details yet.
