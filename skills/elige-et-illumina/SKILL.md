# Elige et Illumina

Instructions: Based on the concept that has been fed to you or generated, pick the most important details that will need the most focused design effort spent upon. These key details should be distinct from one another and should be the most important for a given a concept. Brainstorm 10 key details/categories of details for the concept, then pick the top 5 that would make the concept pop out visually the most.
