# Lege Voluntatem

Instructions: From the user input, analyze the components of the user's request by dissecting what they want and then analyzing each component of their request, then come up with a unique direction/guidance for the next step. E.g. if the user asks for a cool demon, analyze what "cool" and "demon" together means in this case and what would make for a cool demon, then you would come up with a unique meta-direction for the next step. Also, after the analysis, come up with a non-generic criteria for deciding what "best" means: (i.e. darkest, moodiest, most eccentric, etc)
